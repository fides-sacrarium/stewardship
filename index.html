The Church by the Lake

Located near the Garrison Dam and the beautiful waters of Lake Sakakawea, St. Nicholas Church is a place of faith, prayer, and community for all who visit. Whether you are a parishioner, visitor, or traveler passing through, we warmly welcome you.

Welcome

A spiritual home for all

For generations, our church has been a spiritual home at the heart of the community — offering a place for prayer, celebration, and connection through the sacraments, service, and shared faith.

We continue our mission of spreading God's love and compassion to all, and we look forward to welcoming you to St. Nicholas Church.

Whether you are new to the area, returning to the Church, or seeking a spiritual home, we are blessed to have you with us. May your time in our community strengthen your faith, deepen your peace, and draw you ever nearer to the love of God.

"Faith is the light that guides us, and the Church is the home that shelters that light."

Our Mission

Rooted in the Gospel, United in Faith

Rooted in the Gospel of Jesus Christ and guided by the Holy Spirit, our Tri-Parish Community embraces the call to be one family of faith united across three parishes — committed to worshiping God with reverence and joy, growing together in faith formation for all ages, and serving our neighbors with compassion and humility.

Worship

Worshiping God with reverence and joy, especially through the Holy Eucharist and the sacraments.

Formation

Growing together in faith, offering catechesis for children, youth, and adults of all backgrounds.

Service

Serving our neighbors with compassion and humility, reaching out to rural and tribal communities.

Ministry Roles

Reader

Proclaim the First and Second Readings and the Responsorial Psalm during the Liturgy of the Word. A ministry of the spoken Word of God.

Server

Assist the priest at the altar — carrying the cross, candles, and helping prepare the gifts at the Offertory.

Eucharistic Minister

As an Extraordinary Minister of Holy Communion, assist in distributing the Body and Blood of Christ to the faithful.

Gifts & Greeters

Welcome parishioners with a warm smile and bring forward the offertory gifts at the appropriate time during Mass.

Parish Life

Religious Education

Catechesis for children, youth, and adults — forming hearts in the faith of the Church through the Catechesis of the Good Shepherd and Confirmation preparation.

St. Ann's Altar Society

The women of the parish who care for the sanctuary, the linens, and the seasonal beauty of the liturgy — a devoted ministry of love and service.

Knights of Columbus

Our council of Catholic men in service of the Church and the wider community — supporting charitable works and fellowship across the Tri-Parish.

Youth Ministry

Forming the next generation in joyful, sacramental Catholic life — with programs for high school faith formation and retreats throughout the year.

Practical information for ministry volunteers

What to expect

Arrive 10–15 minutes before Mass. If you cannot fulfill your commitment, notify the parish office promptly so a replacement can be arranged in time.

Who can serve?

Baptized Catholics in good standing are welcome. Some roles require prior training and commissioning. Contact the office to learn more about serving.

Our Diocese

Established in 1909, the Diocese of Bismarck serves Catholics across western North Dakota, united in worship, fellowship, and mission.

Questions?

Reach the parish at 235 2nd St. NE, Garrison, ND 58540. We are grateful for every act of service and happy to help you get started.
