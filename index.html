Diocese of Bismarck

A tradition of faithful service

Stewardship is the joyful return of a portion of our time, talent, and treasure. When you serve at Mass, you participate in something sacred — making the liturgy come alive for an entire congregation.

Reader

Proclaim the First and Second Readings and the Responsorial Psalm during the Liturgy of the Word.

Server

Assist the priest at the altar — carrying the cross, candles, and helping prepare the gifts.

Eucharistic Minister

As an Extraordinary Minister, assist in distributing the Body and Blood of Christ to the faithful.

Gifts & Greeters

Welcome parishioners and bring forward the offertory gifts at the appropriate time.

Our Diocese

Established in 1909, the Diocese of Bismarck serves Catholics across western North Dakota. Our parishes are united in worship, fellowship, and a shared call to mission.

What to expect

Arrive 10–15 minutes before Mass. If you cannot fulfill your commitment, notify the parish office promptly so a replacement can be arranged.

Who can serve?

Baptized Catholics in good standing are welcome. Some roles require prior training and commissioning. Contact the office to learn more about requirements.

Questions?

Reach out to the parish office for scheduling, training, or to be added to the regular rotation. We are grateful for every act of service.
